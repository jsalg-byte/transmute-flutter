## Global visual grammar

References [01–26](references/competitor-2026-10-03/) are Liftoff dark phone stills. A slim persistent header has avatar/level and narrow progress track at left, flame/currency in the middle and a contextual action at right; a fixed six-icon bottom bar remains while content scrolls, with a visibly selected destination. Transmute keeps the compact persistent shell but shows progression counters only after real results exist; no invented currency. Today has an underlined equal-width For You / Feed / Discovery row. Ranks has horizontally scrollable underlined Your Rank / Bodygraph / Leagues / Gallery / Calculator / Analysis tabs. Main pages are vertical scrolls with bold section titles, large primary numbers/actions, muted supporting labels, inset rounded cards, and close-packed two/three-up KPI or grid sections. Major cards nearly fill content width with modest edge gutters. Avoid exact pixel claims. Two-column grids stack when large text or narrow width makes them illegible.

Use Transmute's Ledger/Soft geometry, saved palettes, Spectral-plus-sans typography and original alchemical emblems/body art. Keep plain fitness labels: Rank, Level, XP, Streak, Volume. Arcana complements them. Color or animation alone cannot identify status. Preserve semantic text, 44dp targets, focus, 200% text and reduced-motion behavior. Do not copy Liftoff branding, mascot, blue/gold badges, currency/store, shaming text, premium tease or exact copy. [27–28](references/competitor-2026-10-03/) are from a second strawberry-themed nutrition app: their summary and image-card hierarchy is secondary evidence, not a palette/art direction.

The plan's [inventory](COMPETITOR_UPGRADE_PLAN.md#screen-by-screen-reference-inventory) links all 28 stills. References 25–26 show routine entry/previews, **not** sharing or the active set ledger. Reference 15 advertises photo food logging, **not** its recognition UI. Recipe detail, leaderboard tables and import screens are proposed.
